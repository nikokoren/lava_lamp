# Changelog

## v1.1 (2026-11-12)

Now even lavaier.

- Blobs circulate in convection loops instead of bobbing in fixed columns, so they meet and merge sideways too.
- No more jumps at the top and bottom of a blob's travel: tails and drips ease round the turn.
- Rising wax is quick and stretched and sheds a drip; sinking wax is slow and blunt.
- The TRMNL X gets fewer, bigger blobs (a similar physical size to the OG) and a third gray for a rounder look.
- Smoother outlines and gloss, no stray flecks, and about half the SVG per frame.

## v1.0 (2026-10-24)

First release: a full-screen, procedurally generated lava lamp that advances a little with every refresh.
